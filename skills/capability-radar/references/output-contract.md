# Report contract

Produce concise Markdown with these sections.

## 1. Requirement card

State the outcome, audience, inputs/outputs, environment, deployment, budget, privacy/security, license, must-haves, exclusions, priorities, and assumptions.

## 2. Search coverage

Give the as-of date, languages, searched layers, skipped layers with reasons, and important source limitations.

## 3. Market map

Summarize the viable approaches by layer. Show how layers can compose; do not treat an agent prompt, library, MCP server, and complete product as interchangeable.

## 4. Verified shortlist

For each deeply checked candidate include:

- type and direct URL;
- weighted score, confidence, and classification;
- license and deployment model;
- evidence-backed strengths;
- material risks and missing evidence;
- primary evidence links.

## 5. Recommendation

Recommend no more than three primary options. Explain the winner, when another finalist is better, why the rest lost, and whether a composed stack is preferable.

## 6. Proof of concept

Describe the smallest test, success criteria, expected cost/time, and rollback. Ask for confirmation before installing or executing anything.

## 7. Freshness notice

State the research date. If live or primary verification was incomplete, say that the recommendation is not confirmed current and lower confidence accordingly.
